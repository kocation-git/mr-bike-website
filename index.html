Frequently Asked Questions

Got more questions? Get in touch →

How quickly can you come? ↓

We typically offer same-day or next-day slots across Copenhagen. For urgent repairs, call us directly at +45 91 61 00 13 and we'll do our best to get to you within a few hours.

Do you bring all the parts? ↓

Yes! Our vans are stocked with the most common bike parts — tubes, tires, cables, brake pads, chains, and more. For rare or specialty parts, we may need to order ahead — just mention your bike model when booking.

What areas do you cover? ↓

We cover all of Copenhagen and the greater area including Frederiksberg, Amager, Østerbro, Nørrebro, Vesterbro, and surrounding neighborhoods. Not sure? Just ask when booking!

How much does it cost? ↓

Pricing depends on the service. A flat tire repair starts from 199 DKK, brake adjustment from 149 DKK, and a full tune-up from 599 DKK. Parts are charged separately at fair market price. We always provide a quote before starting any work.

Can I book same-day? ↓

Absolutely! Same-day bookings are available subject to mechanic availability. We recommend booking early in the morning for the best chance of a same-day slot. You can also call us directly for faster scheduling.
