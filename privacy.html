Mr. Bike

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Who We Are

Mr. Bike is a mobile bike repair service operating in Copenhagen, Denmark. We can be reached at hello@mrbike.dk or +45 91 61 00 13.

2. What Data We Collect

When you book a repair through our website, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Address (for the service visit)
  • Service requested and preferred date
  • Any additional details you provide

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data exclusively to:

  • Process and confirm your booking
  • Communicate with you about your repair
  • Send appointment reminders
  • Follow up after service completion

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Google reCAPTCHA v3 — to protect our booking form from spam. This service may collect browser and usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps — embedded map on our service area page. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — for typography.

5. Cookies

We use minimal cookies:

  • reCAPTCHA cookies — set by Google to distinguish humans from bots
  • Cookie consent preference — stored in your browser's local storage to remember your choice

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

6. Data Retention

We retain your booking data for up to 12 months after the service date for customer support and record-keeping purposes. You may request deletion at any time.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@mrbike.dk or use the form below.

Request Data Deletion

Enter the email address you used when booking. We will send a confirmation link to verify your identity before deleting your data.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data, including encrypted email transmission and secure server infrastructure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
